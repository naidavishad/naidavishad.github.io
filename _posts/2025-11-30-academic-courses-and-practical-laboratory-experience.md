During the first semester, I studied several subjects that helped build a strong academic foundation. Calculus, taught by Ms. Arshi, enhanced my mathematical thinking and analytical abilities. In the ICT course and ICT Lab, I learned essential computer skills such as Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, document formatting, and professional presentation creation. The Programming Fundamentals Lab gave me the opportunity to apply coding concepts practically by solving programming exercises and developing small programs. I also studied Applied Physics along with its laboratory sessions, where I performed experiments to understand scientific concepts through practical work. These courses together improved both my theoretical understanding and practical skills.
