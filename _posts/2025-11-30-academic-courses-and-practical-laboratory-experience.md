During my first semester at university, I studied several subjects that helped me build a strong academic foundation. Every course introduced me to new knowledge, practical skills, and different ways of thinking. As a first-year student, everything was new for me. The classrooms, laboratories, assignments, presentations, and practical work were very different from my school experience. At first, I felt nervous because I did not know what to expect, but with time I became more comfortable and confident. Every subject played an important role in my learning journey and helped me grow both academically and personally.

One of the main subjects was **Calculus**, taught by **Ms. Arshi**. Mathematics at the university level was much more challenging than what I had studied in school. In the beginning, I found some topics difficult because they required logical thinking, problem-solving skills, and regular practice. We learned about limits, functions, derivatives, integration, and mathematical applications. Ms. Arshi explained every topic step by step and encouraged us to practice as many questions as possible. Although solving mathematical problems was sometimes difficult, I gradually improved my understanding through continuous practice. This course taught me patience, analytical thinking, and the importance of consistency.

Another important subject was **Information and Communication Technology (ICT)**. This course introduced us to the basic concepts of computers and digital technology. Although I had used a computer before, I had never learned many of its professional features. Through this course, I understood how computers are used in education, business, and professional environments. We learned about computer hardware, software, operating systems, networking, internet applications, cybersecurity, and digital communication. The subject helped me understand how technology has become an essential part of modern life.

The **ICT Lab** was one of the most enjoyable parts of the semester because it gave us practical experience. For many students, including me, it was our first experience working in a professional computer laboratory. I still remember entering the computer lab for the first time. Seeing rows of computers, projectors, and networking equipment made me both excited and nervous. I was worried that I might press the wrong key or make a mistake while using the computer. Since everything was new, even simple tasks like logging into the system, saving files in the correct folders, and organizing documents required careful attention.

At first, working in the computer lab felt challenging. Sometimes I could not find the correct software, accidentally closed a program, or forgot where I had saved my files. Small mistakes often made me nervous, but with practice I became more confident. The lab instructors and teachers always encouraged us to keep practicing instead of worrying about mistakes. They reminded us that every beginner faces these challenges and that learning comes through experience.

During the ICT Lab sessions, we learned many practical computer skills that are useful in everyday life. We worked with **Microsoft Word**, where we learned document formatting, page layouts, tables, headers, footers, page numbering, and professional document preparation. We also learned how to create presentations using **Microsoft PowerPoint**. At first, making presentations seemed difficult because I did not know how to organize information or design attractive slides. However, after practicing several presentations, I learned how to create professional slides, insert images, use animations carefully, and present information clearly. These skills have continued to help me in many university assignments and presentations.

One of my favorite practical courses was the **Programming Fundamentals Lab**. This was where we applied the programming concepts that we learned during lectures. Entering the programming lab for the first time was an exciting experience. Every student sat in front of a computer, opened the programming software, and started writing code. Initially, writing even a small program felt difficult because I was unfamiliar with programming syntax and logical thinking. Whenever I wrote my code, I often received error messages that I did not understand.

At first, these errors were frustrating, and I wondered if programming was really meant for me. However, as I continued practicing, I realized that making mistakes is a normal part of learning programming. Every error taught me something new. I learned how to read error messages, identify mistakes, correct my code, and test my programs again. With every successful program, my confidence increased.

The Programming Fundamentals Lab helped me develop many valuable skills. I learned how to write programs, solve logical problems, debug errors, and think systematically. Every lab exercise challenged us to apply the concepts learned in class. Some exercises were easy, while others required careful planning and logical reasoning. Completing these programming tasks gave me a great sense of achievement and motivated me to continue improving my coding skills.

Another important subject during the semester was **Applied Physics** along with its laboratory sessions. Physics helped me understand scientific principles that explain how the world works. We studied topics related to motion, force, electricity, energy, and other physical concepts. While the theoretical lectures explained the concepts, the laboratory sessions allowed us to observe these principles through practical experiments.

The Applied Physics Lab was another completely new experience for me. Before starting each experiment, the instructor explained the objectives, equipment, and safety rules. We worked carefully with different scientific instruments, took measurements, recorded observations, and calculated results. At first, handling laboratory equipment required extra attention because I was afraid of making mistakes. However, after completing several experiments, I became more comfortable with the laboratory environment. These practical activities helped me understand that science is not only about reading books but also about observing and experimenting.

Throughout the semester, every course contributed to my personal and academic development. The combination of lectures, assignments, presentations, laboratory sessions, and practical exercises taught me valuable skills beyond the classroom. I learned how to manage my time, complete assignments before deadlines, work with classmates, solve problems independently, and communicate more confidently.

Looking back, I realize that the first semester was a period of learning, adjustment, and growth. Like every first-year student, I experienced nervousness, excitement, confusion, and curiosity. Entering computer labs for the first time, learning professional software, writing my first computer programs, preparing presentations, solving mathematical problems, and performing scientific experiments were all unforgettable experiences. Every challenge made me stronger, and every practical session increased my confidence.

Overall, the subjects I studied during my first semester laid a strong foundation for my future studies. Calculus improved my analytical thinking, ICT and ICT Lab developed my computer skills, Programming Fundamentals Lab introduced me to coding and problem-solving, and Applied Physics with its laboratory sessions strengthened my scientific understanding. Together, these courses helped transform me from a nervous first-year student into a more confident learner who was ready to face the challenges of university life and continue exploring new knowledge.
