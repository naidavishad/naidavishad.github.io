This semester, I also took on two distinct roles within the Female Core, each bringing its own set of challenges and lessons.

### Head of Tug of War — Female Core

This was my first time managing an event as a Head. The role required coordinating with both students and faculty, handling logistics, ensuring participation, and making decisions on the spot. I had never held this kind of responsibility before, and going into it, I was not entirely sure how it would go. But it did go well. I learned how to communicate clearly under pressure, how to keep things organized when multiple things demand attention at once, and most importantly, how to lead with calm and confidence rather than anxiety. It was a small event in scale but a big step for me personally.

### Director of Photography — Female Core

Alongside my Head role, I also served as Director of Photography. This position gave me the opportunity to document events visually, work creatively, and collaborate with the team in a different capacity. It was a role that required both an eye for detail and the ability to be present at every key moment — something that sharpened my sense of attentiveness and timing throughout the semester.

Together, these two roles gave me a much broader experience of what leadership and teamwork look like in practice — beyond the classroom and beyond the screen.
