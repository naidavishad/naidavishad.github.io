When the semester began, none of us knew each other. Over time, we became friends through lectures, laboratory sessions, assignments, and group discussions. We supported one another during exams, shared knowledge, and spent enjoyable moments together inside and outside the classroom. As the semester came to an end, we appeared in our final examinations. After completing the last paper, our entire class celebrated together with great happiness. We spent quality time, laughed together, and captured many group photographs to preserve those unforgettable memories. Looking back, my first semester was not only about studying but also about building friendships, gaining confidence, overcoming challenges, and creating memories that I will always cherish throughout my university life.

---

**Conclusion**

My first semester was the foundation of my university journey. It taught me much more than academic subjects — it taught me independence, teamwork, confidence, communication, and responsibility. From my first day in the hostel to presenting my first machine learning project and celebrating the completion of final exams, every experience contributed to my personal and professional growth. These moments will always remain a special part of my life and continue to inspire me as I move forward in my academic journey.
