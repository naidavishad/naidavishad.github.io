Among all the experiences this semester brought, co-hosting the IEEE Handover & Farewell Ceremony stands out as one of the most significant. I had the honor of hosting the event alongside Asad Bhai, and together we took the stage for an occasion that carried real weight — both emotionally and professionally.

This was not a small gathering or an informal get-together. It was a proper ceremony, attended by faculty, outgoing leaders, incoming committee members, and students. Standing at that podium, managing the flow of the event, and doing it well — that felt like a turning point. It pushed me beyond my comfort zone and showed me that I was capable of holding my own on a platform that truly mattered. The experience of hosting alongside someone as experienced as Asad Bhai also taught me a great deal about stage presence, audience engagement, and the art of keeping an event moving smoothly.
