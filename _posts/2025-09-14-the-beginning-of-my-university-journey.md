My university journey officially began on 14th September, the day I left my home and moved into the university hostel. It was one of the biggest turning points in my life because it was the first time I had to live away from my family. I felt a mixture of excitement, nervousness, and curiosity about what university life would be like. Although leaving home was emotionally difficult, I was also excited to begin a new chapter filled with learning, personal growth, and new opportunities. This day marked the start of an unforgettable journey that would shape both my academic and personal life.
