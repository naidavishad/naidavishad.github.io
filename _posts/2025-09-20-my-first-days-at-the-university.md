The first few weeks of the semester were dedicated to introductory lectures. During these sessions, our teachers introduced themselves, explained the course outlines, grading policies, and classroom expectations. We also got to know our classmates and explored different departments of the university. Everything felt new, from the classrooms and laboratories to the university environment itself. These introductory classes helped us understand how university education differs from school life and prepared us for the challenges ahead.
