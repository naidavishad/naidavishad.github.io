Living in the hostel was a completely new experience for me. Staying away from home taught me how to become independent and responsible. I learned to manage my daily routine, complete assignments on time, and balance my academic and personal life. Hostel life also gave me the opportunity to meet students from different cities, cultures, and backgrounds. Sharing rooms, helping each other, solving daily problems together, and adapting to a new environment taught me valuable life lessons. These experiences made me more confident, patient, and mature as a person.
