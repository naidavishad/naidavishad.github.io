My entire first year at university was spent under the supervision of **Dr. Bilal**, and when I look back on that time, I feel truly grateful that I had the opportunity to learn from him. The first year of university is one of the most important stages in a student's life because it builds the foundation for future learning. Having a knowledgeable, supportive, and experienced supervisor during this period made a huge difference in my academic journey.

Dr. Bilal was an excellent teacher and mentor. He was highly skilled in the field of **Machine Learning (ML)** and always encouraged students to understand concepts instead of simply memorizing them. His teaching style was unique because he focused on practical learning rather than only theoretical knowledge. Every lecture was well organized, and he explained difficult topics in a simple and easy-to-understand way. Even students who had no previous experience in Machine Learning could understand the concepts with his guidance.

One of the things I admired most about him was his trust in his students. Instead of giving us every answer, he encouraged us to explore, experiment, and solve problems on our own. He believed that the best way to learn was by practicing and making mistakes. This teaching method helped me become more independent and confident. Whenever I faced a challenge, I tried to solve it myself before asking for help, and this habit improved my problem-solving skills.

At the same time, Dr. Bilal always kept an eye on our progress. He gave us enough freedom to work independently, but whenever he noticed that students were losing focus or falling behind, he stepped in and guided us back in the right direction. He regularly set deadlines for assignments and projects, reminding us of the importance of discipline and time management. His balanced approach gave us both freedom and responsibility, which helped us develop as students.

One of the most memorable parts of my first year was learning about Machine Learning under his supervision. Before joining university, I had heard about Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, but I had very little practical knowledge of how these technologies actually worked. Dr. Bilal introduced us to the basics of Machine Learning step by step. He explained concepts such as datasets, features, labels, training data, testing data, and evaluation in a simple way, making it easier for everyone to understand.

The most exciting experience for me was training my **first Machine Learning model**. It was something I had never imagined I would be able to do during my first year. Under Dr. Bilal's guidance, I learned how to prepare a dataset, clean the data, select useful features, train a model, and evaluate its performance. Seeing the model make predictions based on the data was an unforgettable moment. It gave me confidence and motivated me to learn more about Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.

During practical sessions, Dr. Bilal encouraged us to write code ourselves instead of simply copying examples. Whenever we made mistakes, he explained why the error occurred and how to fix it. This approach helped us understand programming concepts more deeply. Every assignment became an opportunity to improve our coding skills, logical thinking, and analytical abilities.

Another quality that made Dr. Bilal an outstanding teacher was his patience. He never discouraged students who asked questions. Instead, he welcomed curiosity and always took the time to explain concepts again if someone did not understand them. His friendly attitude created a comfortable learning environment where students felt confident to participate in discussions and ask for help whenever needed.

Apart from technical knowledge, Dr. Bilal also taught us valuable life skills. He emphasized the importance of consistency, hard work, and continuous learning. He often reminded us that technology changes very quickly, so successful students must always be willing to learn new skills. His advice encouraged me to become a lifelong learner and to keep improving myself every day.

Throughout the year, I also learned how to work on projects, manage deadlines, collaborate with classmates, and present my work confidently. These experiences improved my communication skills, teamwork, and professional attitude. The knowledge and confidence I gained during my first year became a strong foundation for the rest of my university studies.

Looking back, I realize that the first year was much more than simply attending lectures and completing assignments. It was a year of growth, discovery, and personal development. The guidance I received from Dr. Bilal helped me become more responsible, disciplined, and self-motivated. Learning Machine Learning from an expert in the field inspired me to explore modern technologies and strengthened my interest in Artificial Intelligence.

Overall, I consider myself fortunate to have spent my first academic year under the supervision of Dr. Bilal. His expertise in Machine Learning, his practical teaching methods, and his belief in student independence made a lasting impact on my educational journey. From learning the fundamentals of Machine Learning to successfully training my first model, every experience contributed to my growth as a student. The lessons I learned from him continue to guide me in my studies and will remain valuable throughout my academic and professional career. I will always remember my first year with gratitude because it was the beginning of a journey filled with learning, confidence, and new opportunities.
