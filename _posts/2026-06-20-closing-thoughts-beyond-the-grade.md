As Semester 2 draws to a close, my results are still pending. Honestly, I do not know what the numbers will say. There are assignments I wish I had started earlier, nights I wish had gone differently, and moments I would approach with more preparation if I could go back.

But I also know this: no grade will fully capture what this semester actually was.

It was the semester I stepped into leadership for the first time. The semester I hosted a ceremony in front of a full audience and held my ground. The semester I spent a sleepless night alone with a project and came out the other side. The semester I learned what I was made of — not from a textbook, but from experience.

Whatever the grades say, Semester 2 gave me something more lasting: the knowledge that I can handle pressure, carry responsibility, and keep going when it gets hard.

On to Semester 3 — with more to prove, and more to give.
