One of the most interesting subjects of my first semester was Programming Fundamentals (PF), supervised by Dr. Bilal. It was my first experience learning programming in a professional academic environment. Initially, coding seemed difficult because it required logical thinking and problem-solving skills that were completely new to me. However, with regular lectures, lab practice, and continuous guidance from our instructor, I gradually developed confidence in programming. This course taught me how to think logically, solve problems step by step, and write structured computer programs. It became one of the most valuable learning experiences of my first semester.
