One of the most interesting and enjoyable subjects of my first semester was **Programming Fundamentals (PF)**, supervised by **Dr. Bilal**. This course marked the beginning of my programming journey and introduced me to the world of coding in a professional academic environment. Before joining university, I had very little knowledge about programming. I had heard about coding, Artificial Intelligence, and Machine Learning, but I never imagined that I would get the opportunity to learn these technologies during my first semester. Looking back, I consider this course one of the most valuable learning experiences of my university life.

Dr. Bilal was an outstanding teacher who made programming interesting and easy to understand. He had strong knowledge of **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** and **Machine Learning (ML)**, and his passion for these fields inspired many students, including me. His teaching style focused on practical learning rather than simply memorizing theory. Instead of asking us to remember syntax, he encouraged us to understand the logic behind every program. This approach helped us build a strong foundation in programming.

The programming language used in our course was **Python**, which is one of the most popular programming languages in the world. During the first few weeks, everything seemed difficult. Even writing a simple program looked challenging because programming required logical thinking and problem-solving skills that were completely new to me. I often made mistakes in my code, received errors, and struggled to understand why my programs were not working correctly. However, Dr. Bilal always encouraged us not to be afraid of making mistakes. He explained that every programmer learns by debugging errors and practicing regularly.

Every week, we attended both theory lectures and practical laboratory sessions. The lectures helped us understand programming concepts, while the lab sessions gave us the opportunity to apply those concepts by writing real Python programs. These practical sessions became my favorite part of the course because they allowed me to learn through practice. Instead of simply watching demonstrations, we wrote code ourselves, solved programming exercises, and experimented with different solutions.

Throughout the semester, we learned many important Python concepts. We started with simple programs and gradually moved towards more advanced topics. We learned about variables, data types, input and output, arithmetic operations, conditional statements, loops, functions, lists, dictionaries, and file handling. Every new topic expanded our understanding of programming and made us more confident in writing code. Dr. Bilal explained each concept with simple examples before giving us practical tasks to complete independently.

One of the most exciting experiences during the course was working with Python to explore the basics of **Machine Learning**. Although we were first-semester students, Dr. Bilal wanted us to see how programming could be used in modern technologies. Under his guidance, we learned the basic idea of training a Machine Learning model. This experience was completely new for me and made the course even more interesting.

I still remember the excitement of training my **first Machine Learning model**. We learned how to prepare a dataset, load data into Python, process the data, and train a simple model. When the model successfully made predictions, I felt a great sense of achievement. It was amazing to see that the Python code we had written could actually teach a computer to recognize patterns from data. That moment increased my interest in Artificial Intelligence and motivated me to continue learning more about this field.

During laboratory sessions, Dr. Bilal encouraged every student to write code independently instead of copying from classmates. Whenever we encountered errors, he guided us to understand the problem instead of giving us the solution immediately. This teaching method improved our logical thinking, debugging skills, and confidence. By solving problems on our own, we became better programmers and developed the habit of thinking critically.

Another quality that made Dr. Bilal an excellent teacher was his patience and supportive attitude. He always welcomed students' questions and never made anyone feel uncomfortable for asking basic questions. Whether someone needed help with a small syntax error or a complex programming concept, he explained everything calmly and clearly. His friendly behavior created a positive learning environment where students felt comfortable participating in discussions and asking for guidance whenever they needed it.

Apart from programming, Dr. Bilal also taught us valuable lessons about discipline, consistency, and continuous learning. He reminded us that becoming a good programmer requires regular practice. He encouraged us to spend time coding every day because programming is a skill that improves with experience. His advice motivated me to practice Python outside the classroom, solve additional exercises, and explore new programming concepts on my own.

The assignments and lab tasks also played an important role in my learning. Every assignment challenged us to apply what we had learned in class. Although some tasks were difficult, completing them gave me confidence and improved my programming skills. I gradually realized that coding was not only about writing commands but also about thinking logically and finding efficient solutions to problems.

By the end of the semester, I had gained much more than basic programming knowledge. I had learned how to analyze problems, design solutions, write structured Python programs, debug errors, and build simple Machine Learning models. Most importantly, I developed confidence in my ability to learn new technologies. The fear I had at the beginning of the semester disappeared, and it was replaced by curiosity and enthusiasm for programming.

Overall, Programming Fundamentals was one of the most meaningful courses of my first semester. Under the guidance of Dr. Bilal, I built a strong foundation in Python programming and took my first steps into the exciting world of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. His practical teaching style, encouragement, and dedication made learning both enjoyable and effective. From writing my first Python program to training my first Machine Learning model, every experience contributed to my academic growth. The knowledge and skills I gained in this course continue to help me in my university studies, and I will always remember it as one of the most valuable and inspiring parts of my first-semester journey.
