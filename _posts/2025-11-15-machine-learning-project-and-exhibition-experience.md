One of the biggest achievements of my first semester was completing our Machine Learning project. Our team worked on training a dataset using machine learning techniques, understanding the data, and presenting the results. This project introduced me to the practical side of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. After completing the project, we presented it before our teachers and classmates. Later, our work was displayed in the university exhibition, where senior students and faculty members visited our stall and asked us technical questions about our project. Answering their questions improved my confidence, communication skills, and technical knowledge. It was my first experience representing a university project in front of a large audience, making it one of the most memorable moments of my semester.
