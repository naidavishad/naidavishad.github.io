The final weeks of the semester were, without question, the most demanding stretch I have faced so far in university. Within a single week, there were three exhibitions — all requiring preparation, presentation, and performance at the same time.

It was exhausting in a way that is difficult to fully describe. Sleep was short. The days blurred together. Every hour felt like it needed to be accounted for. But there is something remarkable about what pressure like that reveals about a person — and about a team. We pushed through, day and night, supporting each other and keeping ourselves going. By the end of that week, I was tired in every sense of the word, but I was also proud. Not just of what was submitted, but of the fact that we did not fold when it got hard.
